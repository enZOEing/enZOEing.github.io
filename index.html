About

I am a first-year master's student in the NLP Lab at Jeonbuk National University, advised by Prof. Hyun-Je Song.

I am interested in how large language models are evaluated with rubrics, and in using such rubric-based judgments as reward signals for training models on open-ended generation tasks.

Research interests

  • Rubric-based Reward Modeling Rubric-based LLM judgments as reward signals for RL on creative and open-ended text generation
